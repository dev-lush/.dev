### **Effective Date: September 9, 2026**
This Privacy Policy explains how **DBDev** ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Discord application **.dev** ("the App").

By adding or interacting with the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect
We only collect data necessary to provide the features and functionality of the App. Depending on how you interact with the App, we may collect:
- **Discord Identifiers:** User IDs, Server (Guild) IDs, Channel IDs, and Role IDs.
- **Interaction & Command Data:** Logs of slash commands invoked, interaction timestamps, and command parameters provided by the user.
- **Server Configurations:** Custom settings, server prefixes, channel preferences, or permissions set up specifically for the App.
- **Message Data:** Message content in channels where the App is active, processed temporarily to execute commands or automated actions. We do not permanently store message contents unless explicitly saved by a command (e.g., custom tags, support tickets).
- **Diagnostic Logs:** Error reports, API response times, and system usage statistics used to maintain stability.

We do not collect personal real-world identities, email addresses, passwords, payment details, or private messages outside of bot interaction contexts.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected data strictly to:
- Operate, maintain, and deliver the features of **.dev**.
- Store your server preferences and individual user settings.
- Troubleshoot technical bugs, prevent abuse, and optimize bot performance.
- Ensure compliance with Discord's Developer Terms of Service and Developer Policy.
> [!IMPORTANT]
> We never sell, rent, trade, or monetize your data with third parties or advertisers.

## 3. Data Storage & Retention
- **Persistent Data:** Server preferences and stored user data are retained as long as the App remains in your server or until a deletion request is made.
- **Temporary Logs:** System and diagnostic logs are automatically purged after **30 days**.
- **Storage Security:** All stored data is kept on secure databases hosted on [MongoDB Atlas, VPS] with restricted access controls.

## 4. Third-Party Services
The App relies on third-party infrastructure providers to function. Data processed by these services is subject to their respective privacy standards:
- **Discord API:** Provided by Discord Inc. Usage is governed by Discord's Privacy Policy.
- **Hosting / Database Provider:** [bot-hosting.net]

## 5. Your Data Rights & Deletion Requests
You have full control over the data stored about you or your server. You can manage or remove your data by:
- **Removing the App:** Kicking or removing **.dev** from your server immediately stops all future data collection for that server.
- **Submitting a Deletion Request:** Contact us via [Support Email](mailto:thediscordtimes@gmail.com) with your Discord User ID or Server ID to permanently delete all associated database entries.
- Deletion requests are processed within 7 days.

## 6. Children's Privacy
The App is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age (or 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under these ages. If you believe a minor has provided us with data, please contact us for immediate deletion.

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Updates will be reflected by modifying the "Effective Date" at the top of this document. Significant changes will be announced in our support server or via bot announcements.
